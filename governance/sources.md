# Sources

All sources were opened and checked on **8 October 2026**. Laws and guidance change. Check them again before you rely on any statement in this pack.

This is not legal advice.

"Official" means the text comes from the law-maker, the regulator or the standards body. "Secondary" means a law-firm or news summary. We used these only for facts we could not open in an official source, and we say so wherever we rely on them.

## What we could not confirm

- **The exact Official Journal date of Regulation (EU) 2026/1744.** The EUR-Lex page we opened showed the act date (8 July 2026) and its entry into force (27 July 2026), but not the publication date. Two secondary sources give 24 July 2026.
- **Whether the UAE PDPL Executive Regulations have been issued since September 2026.** Our two most recent secondary sources say they have not. One vendor blog mentions a "1 January 2027" compliance deadline, but we found no official source for it, so the pack does not rely on it.
- **The full text of DIFC Regulation 10.** The DIFC legal database page links the Regulations as a PDF, which we did not open. Our description relies on two law-firm summaries. Read the PDF before relying on any detail.
